# POL-MOB-001 — Internal Transfer and Role Change

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Eligibility
1.1 You may apply for an internal move after completing probation and at least 12 months in your current role.
1.2 Exceptions to the 12-month rule need approval from both your current and receiving HRBPs.

## 2. Types of move
2.1 Role change: a new role at the same location.
2.2 Team change: the same or a similar role in a different team at the same location.
2.3 Domestic location change: a move to another UAE site.
2.4 International location change: a move to an outstation outside the UAE. POL-MOB-002 also applies.

## 3. Approvals and timeline
3.1 The receiving manager endorses the move within 5 working days of the request.
3.2 The releasing manager agrees a release date within 7 working days of the request. The notice period between releasing and receiving teams is normally 4 weeks and may not exceed 8 weeks.
3.3 HR Operations approves the transfer within 12 days of the request, once both endorsements are in place.
3.4 HR Operations issues a transfer letter, with any change to grade, salary or allowances, within 2 working days of approval.

## 4. If approvals stall
4.1 If an endorsement is more than 2 working days late, your HRBP follows up with the manager concerned.
4.2 The releasing manager may not block a move that has been endorsed and approved. They may only agree the release date within the limits of §3.2.
4.3 You can see where your move stands at any time in the Employee Journey Companion.

## 5. Your first weeks in the new role
5.1 Systems access is switched on your effective date (POL-IT-001 §3).
5.2 Role training is booked within 10 days (POL-LRN-001 §3).
5.3 Your HRBP holds a 30-day check-in in the new role.

## 6. Pay and grade
6.1 A move to a higher grade takes effect from the move effective date.
6.2 A lateral move keeps your current salary unless the transfer letter states otherwise.
