# POL-IT-001 — IT Equipment and System Access

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Standard equipment
1.1 Office-based employees receive a standard laptop and a company mobile phone. Operational roles receive the equipment specified by their department.
1.2 Equipment is ordered automatically when your onboarding record is created after background clearance.

## 2. New joiner timeline
2.1 Your laptop and phone should be ready on your first working day, and no later than Day 1.
2.2 Your network account, email and core systems (HR self-service, learning portal, intranet) should be active by Day 2.
2.3 Role-specific systems are requested by your manager and provisioned within 3 working days of approval.

## 3. Internal moves
3.1 When you move role or team, access to your new systems is switched on your move effective date.
3.2 Access you no longer need is removed within 5 working days of your move.
3.3 Your existing laptop moves with you unless your new role needs different equipment.

## 4. Service levels
4.1 Equipment issue requests: resolved within 2 working days.
4.2 Access requests: resolved within 1 working day of approval.
4.3 Requests outside service level are escalated automatically to the IT Service Delivery Manager after 2 times the service level.

## 5. While you wait
5.1 If your laptop is not issued by Day 1, the IT Service Desk can provide a temporary loan device within 4 working hours on request.
5.2 Your manager should not expect you to complete system-based tasks until access is in place.

## 6. Security
6.1 Never share your password. IT will never ask for it.
6.2 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk immediately.
