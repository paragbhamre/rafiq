# POL-MOB-002 — Relocation Support for Internal Moves

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Relocation assessment
1.1 Global Mobility assesses your relocation package within 4 days of transfer approval. They confirm your entitlements in writing.
1.2 The assessment covers housing, shipment of household goods, schooling support (international only), work permits and the relocation allowance.

## 2. Domestic moves (within the UAE)
2.1 Relocation allowance: see POL-ALW-001 §4.
2.2 Temporary housing: up to 21 nights (POL-ACC-001 §3.2).
2.3 Removal of household goods by a company-appointed mover, up to one standard truckload.

## 3. International moves (to an outstation)
3.1 Global Mobility applies for your destination work permit or visa. Target: the permit is issued at least 4 days before your move effective date.
3.2 Your move effective date will not be confirmed until the work permit is issued. You must not travel to work at the outstation without a valid permit.
3.3 Shipment allowance: up to a 20 ft container for family moves, or 5 cubic metres for single employees.
3.4 Temporary housing at the destination: up to 45 nights (POL-ACC-001 §3.3).
3.5 Schooling support for dependent children is covered under the outstation terms set out in your transfer letter.

## 4. Payment timing
4.1 The relocation allowance is paid at least 5 days before your move effective date.
4.2 Payroll service level: allowance payment requests are processed within 5 working days of Global Mobility approval.

## 5. If something is late
5.1 If your work permit, allowance or housing is late, the Global Mobility Lead and your HRBP are informed. They agree whether the move effective date needs to change.
5.2 A move date is only changed with your agreement and that of both managers.
