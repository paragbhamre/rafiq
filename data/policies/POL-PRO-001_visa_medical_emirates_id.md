# POL-PRO-001 — Employment Visa, Medical Test and Emirates ID

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy and not legal or immigration advice.

## 1. Who this applies to
1.1 This policy applies to new joiners and transferring employees who need the company to sponsor their UAE employment visa and residency.
1.2 UAE nationals and GCC nationals do not need an employment visa. The steps in sections 3 to 5 do not apply to them.

## 2. Documents you must provide
2.1 Within 3 working days of being asked, provide: a passport copy valid for at least 6 months, a passport photo on a white background, attested educational certificates where the role requires them, and any previous UAE visa cancellation papers.
2.2 If documents are missing or unclear, PRO will set your request to "Pending information" and tell you exactly what is needed. The visa timeline pauses until the documents are received.

## 3. Visa application
3.1 PRO will submit your employment visa application within 2 working days of background clearance and receipt of complete documents.
3.2 Target: the application is submitted no later than 28 days before your joining date.

## 4. Medical fitness test
4.1 After your entry permit is issued, PRO will book a medical fitness test for you at an approved centre. You will receive the date, time and location.
4.2 Target: the medical is completed no later than 18 days before your joining date (or within 5 working days of arrival if you enter on an entry permit).
4.3 If your medical result is delayed beyond 5 working days, PRO will chase the medical centre and update you.

## 5. Visa issue and Emirates ID
5.1 Target: the visa is issued no later than 10 days before your joining date.
5.2 PRO will apply for your Emirates ID and residency stamping within 7 days of your joining date. Biometrics appointments are booked by PRO.

## 6. Delays and escalation
6.1 PRO service level: each PRO request is acknowledged within 1 working day and actioned within 5 working days, excluding time waiting for government authorities.
6.2 If a visa step is stalled and your joining date is within 14 days, the case is escalated to the PRO Team Lead and your HRBP. Your HRBP will agree with your hiring manager whether the joining date needs to move.
6.3 Your joining date is only changed with your agreement. You will not be asked to travel without a valid entry permit.

## 7. Costs
7.1 All visa, medical and Emirates ID fees for the employee are paid by the company.
7.2 Dependants' visa costs are covered by POL-ALW-001 §5.
