# POL-ONB-001 — Onboarding and Induction

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Purpose
1.1 This policy sets out what every new joiner can expect from the date they accept an offer until the end of their first 90 days.
1.2 It defines who is responsible for each onboarding step and the target time for completing it.

## 2. Before your first day
2.1 Talent Acquisition will confirm your background and reference checks at least 30 days before your joining date.
2.2 If you need a UAE employment visa, Government Relations (PRO) will start the application within 2 working days of background clearance. See POL-PRO-001.
2.3 If you are relocating from abroad, HR Operations will issue your joining ticket at least 7 days before your joining date. See POL-TRV-001.
2.4 If you are eligible for staff accommodation, Facilities will confirm your housing at least 5 days before arrival. See POL-ACC-001.

## 3. Your first week
3.1 Your laptop and mobile phone should be issued on your first working day, and no later than Day 1. See POL-IT-001.
3.2 Network, email and core system access should be active by Day 2.
3.3 Your staff ID and building or airside pass should be issued by Day 3. See POL-SEC-001.
3.4 Your line manager will assign an onboarding buddy by Day 1. The buddy is your first point of contact for day-to-day questions.
3.5 You will attend corporate induction by Day 5. If you miss the scheduled session, L&D will rebook you into the next available session within 5 working days.

## 4. Your first month
4.1 Mandatory compliance training must be booked by Day 7 and completed within 30 days. See POL-LRN-001.
4.2 Your manager will agree probation objectives with you by Day 30. See POL-ONB-002.
4.3 Your HR Business Partner (HRBP) will hold a 30-day check-in with you.

## 5. If something is late
5.1 If any step above is more than 2 working days late, you may raise a service request or ask the Employee Journey Companion to raise one for you.
5.2 Unresolved delays are escalated to the owner team's manager and your HRBP under POL-SRV-001.
5.3 A delay in onboarding steps must never be held against you in probation or performance assessment.

## 6. Contacts
6.1 Your HRBP is named in your joining letter and in the Employee Journey Companion.
6.2 General onboarding questions: HR Operations, via the HR service portal.
