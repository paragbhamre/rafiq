# POL-ALW-001 — Joining, Housing and Relocation Allowances

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy. All amounts are illustrative.

## 1. Settling-in allowance (new joiners from abroad)
1.1 Single employees: AED 5,000 once only.
1.2 Employees relocating with family: AED 8,000 once only.
1.3 Paid with your first salary.

## 2. Housing allowance
2.1 Employees in grades G5 and above, and employees in grades G1 to G4 who choose not to take company accommodation, receive a monthly housing allowance.
2.2 Monthly amounts: G1–G2 AED 4,500; G3–G4 AED 6,500; G5–G6 AED 9,000; G7 and above as stated in the contract.
2.3 Housing allowance starts from your joining date and is paid monthly with salary.
2.4 You cannot receive housing allowance and company accommodation at the same time.

## 3. Transport allowance
3.1 Employees not on a company transport route receive AED 1,000 a month.
3.2 Shift-based operational staff at airport sites may use company transport instead.

## 4. Relocation allowance (internal moves)
4.1 Domestic location change: AED 3,000 once only.
4.2 International location change: one month's basic salary, up to AED 25,000, once only.
4.3 Paid at least 5 days before your move effective date (POL-MOB-002 §4.1).
4.4 Not payable for role or team changes at the same location.

## 5. Dependants
5.1 Visa and medical costs for up to 3 sponsored dependants are covered by the company.

## 6. Questions about amounts
6.1 Your exact entitlements are in your offer or transfer letter. If the letter and this policy differ, the letter applies.
6.2 For questions about your specific payment, contact Payroll through the HR service portal.
