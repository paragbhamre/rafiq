# POL-ONB-002 — Probation Period

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Length of probation
1.1 The probation period is 6 months from the joining date, unless your contract states otherwise.
1.2 Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, with written reasons from your manager and approval from your HRBP.

## 2. Objectives
2.1 Your line manager must agree written probation objectives with you within 30 days of joining.
2.2 If objectives have not been set by Day 35, you or your HRBP may raise this. Your HRBP will follow up with your manager.

## 3. Reviews
3.1 You will have an informal check-in at 30 days and a formal mid-point review at 3 months.
3.2 The probation confirmation decision is made by your line manager and approved by your HRBP.

## 4. What is not used in probation decisions
4.1 Delays caused by onboarding steps outside your control (for example, late IT equipment, visa processing or access issues) must not count against you.
4.2 Your conversations with the Employee Journey Companion, and any pulse survey feedback you give, are never used in probation, performance or disciplinary decisions. See POL-PRV-001.

## 5. Questions
5.1 For questions about your probation, contact your line manager or HRBP. The Employee Journey Companion can explain this policy, but it cannot make or predict probation decisions.
