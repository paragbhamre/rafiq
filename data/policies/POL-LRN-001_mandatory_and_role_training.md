# POL-LRN-001 — Mandatory and Role Training

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Corporate induction
1.1 All new joiners attend corporate induction by Day 5.
1.2 Induction runs every Monday and Wednesday. If you miss your session, L&D rebooks you within 5 working days.

## 2. Mandatory compliance training
2.1 Mandatory modules: Code of Conduct, Information Security, Data Protection, Health and Safety, and Anti-Bribery.
2.2 These must be booked by Day 7 and completed within 30 days of joining.
2.3 E-learning modules are on the learning portal. You need system access (POL-IT-001 §2.2) to take them.

## 3. Role training after an internal move
3.1 Role-specific training is booked within 10 days of your move effective date.
3.2 Your receiving manager identifies the training needed. L&D books it.

## 4. Service level
4.1 Training booking requests are actioned within 3 working days.

## 5. If you cannot complete training on time
5.1 If missing system access or equipment prevents you from completing mandatory training, the 30-day deadline is extended automatically. You will not be marked non-compliant.
