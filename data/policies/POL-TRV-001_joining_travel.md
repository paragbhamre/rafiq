# POL-TRV-001 — Joining Travel and Baggage

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Joining ticket
1.1 If you are relocating from outside the UAE, the company provides a one-way Economy class ticket from your home city to Dubai on your joining date.
1.2 Eligible family members on your visa sponsorship receive tickets on the same terms.
1.3 HR Operations issues the ticket no later than 7 days before your joining date, and only after your entry permit is issued (where one is needed).

## 2. Excess baggage
2.1 Single employees: up to 30 kg of additional checked baggage.
2.2 Employees relocating with family: up to 60 kg of additional checked baggage.

## 3. Arrival
3.1 Airport pick-up to your accommodation is arranged for arrivals at Dubai International (DXB).
3.2 If your flight changes, tell HR Operations at least 48 hours before departure.

## 4. Changes to travel dates
4.1 If your joining date moves (for example, because of a visa delay), HR Operations will reissue your ticket at no cost to you.
