# POL-ACC-001 — Staff Accommodation and Temporary Housing

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Eligibility
1.1 Company accommodation is offered to employees in grades G1 to G4 who are relocating from outside the UAE, and to cabin crew and flight deck crew under their contract terms.
1.2 Employees in grades G5 and above receive a housing allowance instead. See POL-ALW-001 §2.
1.3 Your offer letter states whether you are accommodation-eligible. This is shown as `accommodation_eligible` in your journey record.

## 2. Assignment timeline
2.1 Facilities will confirm your accommodation address and move-in details no later than 5 days before your arrival.
2.2 If permanent accommodation is not ready, Facilities will provide furnished temporary housing (serviced apartment or hotel) from your arrival date.

## 3. Temporary housing
3.1 New joiners: up to 30 nights of temporary housing.
3.2 Domestic relocation: up to 21 nights.
3.3 International relocation to an outstation: up to 45 nights.
3.4 Extensions beyond these limits need approval from the Facilities Manager and your HRBP.

## 4. If your accommodation is not confirmed
4.1 If you have not received your accommodation details 3 days before arrival, raise a Facilities request or ask the Employee Journey Companion to do so.
4.2 Facilities service level: accommodation requests are actioned within 3 working days. Requests within 3 days of arrival are treated as High priority.
4.3 Unconfirmed accommodation within 2 days of arrival is escalated to the Facilities Manager.

## 5. Maintenance
5.1 Report maintenance issues through the Facilities portal. Urgent issues (no water, power or air-conditioning) are attended within 4 hours.
