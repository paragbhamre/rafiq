# POL-PRV-001 — Employee Data, Consent and the Employee Journey Companion

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy and not legal advice.

## 1. Purpose of the companion
1.1 The Employee Journey Companion works for you. It helps you see where you are in your onboarding or move, answers policy questions, raises requests for you and connects you to the right person when something is stuck.
1.2 It is not a monitoring tool. It does not report on you to your manager.

## 2. What data it uses
2.1 Without asking you: your journey stage, journey events, your own service requests, and your basic employment details (role, location, manager, HRBP).
2.2 Only with your opt-in consent: your pulse survey responses and comments, and any analysis of how you are feeling about your journey.
2.3 It does not use performance ratings, disciplinary records, medical details or private messages.

## 3. Consent
3.1 Pulse feedback is optional. You choose whether to opt in, and you can withdraw consent at any time in the companion.
3.2 When you withdraw consent, your pulse responses are no longer used for any purpose. They are deleted within 30 days.
3.3 Not opting in has no effect on the service you receive.

## 4. Seeing your data
4.1 You can see everything the companion holds about you, including the signals it has flagged, in the "My data" view.
4.2 You can ask for an incorrect record to be corrected through your HRBP.

## 5. Who can see what
5.1 Your manager can see the progress of your journey steps (for example, "IT kit issued"). Your manager cannot see your conversations with the companion, your pulse responses or any sentiment analysis.
5.2 Your HRBP sees a case summary only when the companion hands off an issue to them. You are told when this happens and what is shared.
5.3 Aggregated, anonymised reports (for example, which steps stall most often) are produced only for groups of 10 or more employees.

## 6. Human oversight
6.1 Any action that affects you, such as changing your joining or move date or escalating a case, is taken by a named human owner, never by the companion alone.
6.2 Companion conversations, pulse feedback and companion-generated signals are never used in performance, probation, promotion or disciplinary decisions.

## 7. Accuracy
7.1 Policy answers show the policy and section they come from. If the companion is unsure, it says so and offers to connect you to a person.

## 8. Retention
8.1 Conversation history is kept for 90 days, then deleted. You can delete your conversation history at any time.
