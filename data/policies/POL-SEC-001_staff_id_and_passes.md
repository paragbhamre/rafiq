# POL-SEC-001 — Staff ID and Access Passes

> SYNTHETIC MOCK POLICY for prototype testing. Not a real Emirates policy.

## 1. Staff ID
1.1 Every employee receives a photo staff ID card. It must be worn visibly at all company sites.
1.2 New joiners receive their staff ID by Day 3. Until then, Security issues a temporary visitor pass each day at reception.

## 2. Airside and restricted-area passes
2.1 Roles that need airside or restricted-area access require an airport authority pass. Security submits the application after your staff ID is issued.
2.2 Airport authority processing can take up to 15 working days and is outside company control. Your manager will arrange escorted access in the meantime.

## 3. Moves between locations
3.1 If you move to a different site, Security updates your building access on your move effective date.
3.2 International outstations issue local passes under local airport rules. Global Mobility will brief you.

## 4. Service level
4.1 Staff ID requests are resolved within 2 working days of your photo being received.
4.2 Lost cards: report immediately. Replacements are issued within 1 working day.
